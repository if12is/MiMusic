<b>MiMusic</b> is a maintained fork of ViMusic for streaming music from YouTube Music.

<b>Features:</b>

* Arabic RTL as the default interface, with English available
* Search and play any song or video from YouTube Music
* Background playback with a glossy lock-screen and Dynamic Island player
* Download songs for offline playback
* In-app updater from GitHub Releases
* Search for songs, albums, artists, videos and playlists
* Bookmark artists and albums
* Import playlists
* Lyrics and synchronized lyrics
* Open YouTube/YouTube Music links
* Local playlists and queue management
* Favorites and Offline built-in playlists
* Sleep timer
* Skip silence
* Persistent queue
* Loudness/audio normalization
* Android Auto
